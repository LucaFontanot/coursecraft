# M28 Bug Hunt — 2026-11-22

## Step h1: Player outline drawer opens inside the player, not at the screen edge

- **What was wrong:** When opening the outline drawer (Menu, Resources, Glossary, Transcript) in the desktop preview (where the preview modal is centered and not at `x = 0`) or in a non-fullscreen/letterboxed player, the drawer slid in from `full.left() - DRAWER_WIDTH` (reaching over the editor UI or outside the player area) instead of being clipped to the player area's left edge.
- **Root cause:** `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:2583` and `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:2597` (`draw_drawer`) called `ui.painter().with_clip_rect(rect)` and `child.set_clip_rect(rect)` using the unclipped sliding `drawer_rect(full, progress)` (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:2555`), whose `left()` is `< full.left()` while `progress < 1.0`. This overrode any parent clip rect (such as the preview modal's clip rect) and painted outside `full`. Additionally, `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:722` stored the unclipped `drawer` rect in `last_drawer_rect` while closing.
- **Fix:**
  - Passed `full` (the player area rect) into `draw_drawer` (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:696`, `2571`) and clipped both the background painter and child `Ui` to `ui.clip_rect().intersect(full).intersect(rect)` (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:2586-2603`), painting the drawer background over `clip` instead of the unclipped `rect`.
  - Intersected `last_drawer_rect` with `full` when closing (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:722-724`).
  - Extracted `Playing::show_ui` in `apps/coursecraft-player/src/app.rs:1031` so the published player UI can be tested headlessly.
- **Tests added:**
  - `coursecraft-render`: `player_frame::tests::drawer_clips_to_player_area_at_offset_rect_during_entire_animation` (Menu, Resources, Glossary, Transcript tabs across offset player areas; first, middle, and settled frames) and `player_frame::tests::drawer_clips_to_player_area_at_letterboxed_and_fullscreen_sizes` (wider-than-aspect, taller-than-aspect, and 16:9 fullscreen).
  - `coursecraft-ui-egui`: `preview_drawer_tests::preview_drawer_clips_to_preview_window_across_sizes_and_animation` and `preview_drawer_tests::preview_drawer_with_variables_panel_stays_inside_window`.
  - `coursecraft-player`: `tests::player_drawer_clips_to_player_area_at_letterboxed_and_fullscreen_sizes`.
- **Left undone:** None.

## Step h2: Shape outlines are even all the way round and pointed corners are not cut

- **What was wrong:** Rotated or flipped shapes (such as 90°/180° block arrows, flipped pentagons, and flipped callouts) had their outlines clipped to the shape's bounding box, halving the outline thickness on the sides and slicing pointed tips flat. Additionally, sharp acute corners were beveled at miter limit 4.0 instead of PowerPoint's default 8.0 (`<a:miter lim="800000"/>`), and `<a:round/>` / `<a:bevel/>` / `<a:miter lim="..."/>` line joins were not imported or rendered.
- **Root cause:**
  1. `crates/render/src/paint.rs:1661-1680` (`paint_items`) clipped any `transformed` item (`rotation != 0.0 || item.flip_h || item.flip_v`) to `transformed_bounds(screen_rect.expand(effect_margin), ...)` even when `item.clip` was `None`, which did not account for centred outline half-widths or miter tips extending past `screen_rect`.
  2. `crates/render/src/shape_fill.rs:781` hardcoded `MITER_LIMIT: f32 = 4.0` and had no `LineJoin::Round` or `LineJoin::Bevel` support.
  3. `crates/course/src/model.rs:2736` (`Outline`) and `crates/pptx/src/lib.rs:5101` (`extract_outline`) did not model or parse `<a:round/>`, `<a:bevel/>`, or `<a:miter lim="..."/>`.
- **Fix:**
  - Updated `crates/render/src/paint.rs` (`paint_items`) so `item_painter` only clips to `item.clip` when present and otherwise uses `slide_painter.clone()`, matching untransformed items so outlines and miter tips are never clipped to the shape's box.
  - Added `LineJoin` (`Miter`, `Round`, `Bevel`) and `miter_limit: Option<f32>` to `Outline` (`crates/course/src/model.rs`, validated in `crates/course/src/validate.rs`) and `ResolvedOutline` (`crates/render/src/compose.rs`).
  - Updated `crates/render/src/shape_fill.rs` with `DEFAULT_MITER_LIMIT = 8.0`, `StrokeJoin`, `stroke_contours_with_join`, `stroke_path_with_join`, and `corner_stations` support for `Miter`, `Round` (arc stations), and `Bevel`, wired through `paint.rs` and `path_geometry.rs`.
  - Updated `crates/pptx/src/lib.rs` (`extract_join_from_ln`) to parse `<a:round/>`, `<a:bevel/>`, and `<a:miter lim="..."/>` (defaulting to miter limit 8.0).
- **Tests added:**
  - `coursecraft-render`: `outline_tests::rectangle_outline_thickness_equal_on_all_sides_and_rotations`, `outline_tests::sharp_shapes_preserve_outline_area_and_uncut_tips_across_rotations_flips_and_widths`, and `outline_tests::line_join_miter_round_and_bevel_behave_distinctly_on_acute_tip`.
  - `coursecraft-pptx`: `m26_fidelity_tests::imports_line_join_and_miter_limit`.
- **Left undone:** None.

## Step h3: Preview window: darker backdrop, resizable, full screen

- **What was wrong:**
  1. The desktop preview backdrop used `egui::Modal`'s default translucent dimming, letting the editor's slides, timeline, and panels show through clearly behind the preview.
  2. The desktop preview was capped at 960×640, had no edge/corner resize handles, did not remember its size during the session, and had no full-screen mode (title-bar button or F11 / Cmd+Ctrl+F).
- **Root cause:**
  - `crates/ui-egui/src/preview.rs:753-756` used `egui::Modal::new(...)` with default backdrop color and hardcoded `.clamp(320.0, 960.0)` / `.clamp(240.0, 640.0)` size caps, with no resize handles, session size persistence, or fullscreen state.
  - `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs` (`PlayerFrame`) exposed `strip_rect()`, `drawer_rect()`, etc., but not `slide_rect()`.
- **Fix:**
  - Replaced `egui::Modal` in `crates/ui-egui/src/preview.rs` with a full-screen `preview_backdrop` `Area` (`PREVIEW_BACKDROP_COLOR = Color32::from_black_alpha(204)`, ~80% black in both light and dark themes) + `preview_window` `Area` + 8 perimeter `preview_resize_grips` (`TopLeft`, `TopRight`, `BottomLeft`, `BottomRight`, `Top`, `Bottom`, `Left`, `Right`) clamped between `(PREVIEW_MIN_WIDTH, PREVIEW_MIN_HEIGHT)` (`480×320`) and the app window shrunk by `PREVIEW_MARGIN` (`20.0` per side).
  - Stored `preview_size: Option<egui::Vec2>` on `UiState` (`crates/ui-egui/src/lib.rs`) and synced it in `open_preview_at` and `CourseCraftEditor::show` so the non-fullscreen preview size is remembered for the session.
  - Added `fullscreen: bool` to `Preview`, a Full screen toggle button (`Icon::CornersOut` / `Icon::CornersIn`) in the preview title bar, `F11` / `Cmd+Ctrl+F` shortcuts while the preview has focus, `Esc` exiting full screen before closing on a second `Esc`, and backdrop clicks closing only when not full screen.
  - Added `PlayerFrame::slide_rect(&self) -> Option<Rect>` in `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs` and verified the published web player (`apps/coursecraft-player`) scales, letterboxes without cropping, and adjusts the drawer and log panel across window sizes.
- **Tests added:**
  - `coursecraft-ui-egui` (`crates/ui-egui/src/preview_window_tests.rs`): `preview_backdrop_dims_to_80_percent_black_in_dark_and_light_themes`, `preview_window_resizes_from_all_edges_and_corners_with_min_max_clamps`, `preview_fullscreen_toggle_and_esc_order`, `preview_slide_scales_and_variables_panel_fits_at_three_sizes`.
  - `coursecraft-player` (`apps/coursecraft-player/src/tests.rs`): `player_scales_letterboxes_and_places_drawer_and_log_panel_across_window_sizes`.
- **Left undone:** None.
