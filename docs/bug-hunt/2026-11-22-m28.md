# M28 Bug Hunt — 2026-11-22

## Step h1: Player outline drawer opens inside the player, not at the screen edge

- **What was wrong:** When opening the outline drawer (Menu, Resources, Glossary, Transcript) in the desktop preview (where the preview modal is centered and not at `x = 0`) or in a non-fullscreen/letterboxed player, the drawer slid in from `full.left() - DRAWER_WIDTH` (reaching over the editor UI or outside the player area) instead of being clipped to the player area's left edge.
- **Root cause:** `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:2583` and `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:2597` (`draw_drawer`) called `ui.painter().with_clip_rect(rect)` and `child.set_clip_rect(rect)` using the unclipped sliding `drawer_rect(full, progress)` (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:2555`), whose `left()` is `< full.left()` while `progress < 1.0`. This overrode any parent clip rect (such as the preview modal's clip rect) and painted outside `full`. Additionally, `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:722` stored the unclipped `drawer` rect in `last_drawer_rect` while closing.
- **Fix:**
  - Passed `full` (the player area rect) into `draw_drawer` (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:696`, `2571`) and clipped both the background painter and child `Ui` to `ui.clip_rect().intersect(full).intersect(rect)` (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:2586-2603`), painting the drawer background over `clip` instead of the unclipped `rect`.
  - Intersected `last_drawer_rect` with `full` when closing (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:722-724`).
  - Extracted `Playing::show_ui` in `apps/coursecraft-player/src/app.rs:1031` so the published player UI can be tested headlessly.
- **Tests added:**
  - `coursecraft-render`: `player_frame::tests::drawer_clips_to_player_area_at_offset_rect_during_entire_animation` (Menu, Resources, Glossary, Transcript tabs across offset player areas; first, middle, and settled frames) and `player_frame::tests::drawer_clips_to_player_area_at_letterboxed_and_fullscreen_sizes` (wider-than-aspect, taller-than-aspect, and 16:9 fullscreen).
  - `coursecraft-ui-egui`: `preview_drawer_tests::preview_drawer_clips_to_preview_window_across_sizes_and_animation` and `preview_drawer_tests::preview_drawer_with_variables_panel_stays_inside_window`.
  - `coursecraft-player`: `tests::player_drawer_clips_to_player_area_at_letterboxed_and_fullscreen_sizes`.
- **Left undone:** None.

## Step h2: Shape outlines are even all the way round and pointed corners are not cut

- **What was wrong:** Rotated or flipped shapes (such as 90°/180° block arrows, flipped pentagons, and flipped callouts) had their outlines clipped to the shape's bounding box, halving the outline thickness on the sides and slicing pointed tips flat. Additionally, sharp acute corners were beveled at miter limit 4.0 instead of PowerPoint's default 8.0 (`<a:miter lim="800000"/>`), and `<a:round/>` / `<a:bevel/>` / `<a:miter lim="..."/>` line joins were not imported or rendered.
- **Root cause:**
  1. `crates/render/src/paint.rs:1661-1680` (`paint_items`) clipped any `transformed` item (`rotation != 0.0 || item.flip_h || item.flip_v`) to `transformed_bounds(screen_rect.expand(effect_margin), ...)` even when `item.clip` was `None`, which did not account for centred outline half-widths or miter tips extending past `screen_rect`.
  2. `crates/render/src/shape_fill.rs:781` hardcoded `MITER_LIMIT: f32 = 4.0` and had no `LineJoin::Round` or `LineJoin::Bevel` support.
  3. `crates/course/src/model.rs:2736` (`Outline`) and `crates/pptx/src/lib.rs:5101` (`extract_outline`) did not model or parse `<a:round/>`, `<a:bevel/>`, or `<a:miter lim="..."/>`.
- **Fix:**
  - Updated `crates/render/src/paint.rs` (`paint_items`) so `item_painter` only clips to `item.clip` when present and otherwise uses `slide_painter.clone()`, matching untransformed items so outlines and miter tips are never clipped to the shape's box.
  - Added `LineJoin` (`Miter`, `Round`, `Bevel`) and `miter_limit: Option<f32>` to `Outline` (`crates/course/src/model.rs`, validated in `crates/course/src/validate.rs`) and `ResolvedOutline` (`crates/render/src/compose.rs`).
  - Updated `crates/render/src/shape_fill.rs` with `DEFAULT_MITER_LIMIT = 8.0`, `StrokeJoin`, `stroke_contours_with_join`, `stroke_path_with_join`, and `corner_stations` support for `Miter`, `Round` (arc stations), and `Bevel`, wired through `paint.rs` and `path_geometry.rs`.
  - Updated `crates/pptx/src/lib.rs` (`extract_join_from_ln`) to parse `<a:round/>`, `<a:bevel/>`, and `<a:miter lim="..."/>` (defaulting to miter limit 8.0).
- **Tests added:**
  - `coursecraft-render`: `outline_tests::rectangle_outline_thickness_equal_on_all_sides_and_rotations`, `outline_tests::sharp_shapes_preserve_outline_area_and_uncut_tips_across_rotations_flips_and_widths`, and `outline_tests::line_join_miter_round_and_bevel_behave_distinctly_on_acute_tip`.
  - `coursecraft-pptx`: `m26_fidelity_tests::imports_line_join_and_miter_limit`.
- **Left undone:** None.

## Step h5: Bold/Italic/Underline buttons show the selection's real style; underline toggles cleanly

- **What was wrong:**
  1. Selecting non-italic/non-underlined text right after an italic or underlined run caused the Bold/Italic/Underline/Strikethrough/Superscript/Subscript buttons in the inspector (and `Cmd+B/I/U` in inline editing) to show the previous neighbour's style instead of the selected characters' style, and to ignore the rest of a multi-run selection.
  2. Toggling a style at a caret with no selection either did nothing (`Cmd+B/I/U`) or styled the entire text object (inspector buttons), instead of setting a pending style for the next typed characters.
  3. Toggling a style ON and back OFF left explicit overrides (e.g. `underline: Some(false)`) on runs instead of clearing overrides that match the base `TextStyle`, preventing `normalize_runs` from merging runs back together and leaving fragmented runs across repeated toggles.
- **Root cause:**

  2. `crates/course/src/text.rs:219` (`apply_style_to_range`) and `crates/engine/src/commands.rs:7636` (`text_style_range`) did not strip overrides that matched `parts.style`, so `Some(false)` never collapsed back to `None` to merge with adjacent plain runs.
- **Fix:**
  - **`coursecraft-course` & `coursecraft-engine`** (`crates/course/src/text.rs`, `crates/course/src/lib.rs`, `crates/engine/src/commands.rs`): Added `apply_style_to_range_with_base` and `strip_redundant_overrides`, and wired `text_style_range` to pass `Some(parts.style)` so toggling styles on and back off merges runs cleanly back to the original structure.
  - **`coursecraft-ui-egui` style queries & pending caret style** (`crates/ui-egui/src/properties.rs`, `crates/ui-egui/src/canvas.rs`):
    - Added `run_at_caret`, `style_for_range`, and `effects_for_range` so non-empty selections check every overlapping character in `[start, end)` (active only if every selected character has the style; neighbours outside `[start, end)` never count), whole-object mode checks `(0, total_chars)`, and caret mode checks the character before the caret (or the first run at `0`).
    - Added `PendingCaretStyle` on `PropertiesState` so toggling B/I/U/S/`x²`/`x₂` with a caret and no selection (via inspector buttons or `Cmd+B/I/U`) updates the button state and applies the pending style to the next typed characters.
- **Tests added:**
  - `coursecraft-course` (`crates/course/src/tests.rs`): `apply_style_to_range_with_base_merges_cleanly_on_off_on`.
  - `coursecraft-engine` (`crates/engine/src/tests.rs`): `text_style_range_toggles_cleanly_and_undo_restores_exact_runs`.

  - `coursecraft-ui-egui` (`crates/ui-egui/src/style_toggles_tests.rs`): `style_query_ignores_neighbours_and_handles_caret_boundaries`, `inspector_style_buttons_reflect_selection_and_toggle_cleanly_with_undo`, `selection_across_two_paragraphs_toggles_and_restores`, `caret_pending_style_applies_to_next_typed_characters`.

- **What was wrong:** The bottom control bar (play, replay, seek bar, volume, captions, speed) was drawn on top of the bottom of the slide, hiding slide text boxes and video subtitles near the bottom edge.
- **Root cause:** `crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:614` (`PlayerFrame::show`) computed `slide_rect(full, player)` against the entire player rect `full`, and `update_visibility` (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs:994`) defaulted to an auto-hiding floating overlay over the slide.
- **Fix:**
  - **`coursecraft-render` (`crates/render/src/player_frame.rs`)**: Added `pub overlay_strip: bool` (default `false`) and `STRIP_GAP_ABOVE` (`8.0`). By default, when a control strip is laid out (`!self.overlay_strip` and `layout.is_some()`), `PlayerFrame::show` splits `full` at `layout.rect.top() - STRIP_GAP_ABOVE` so `slide_rect` (and therefore `SlideView`, video elements via `MediaDriver::layout_videos`, captions, layers, lightboxes, and focus outlines) fits strictly above the control bar, while the strip remains visible without auto-hiding on idle. When no strip is shown (e.g. course ended or tiny height below `STRIP_HEIGHT`), the slide receives `full`. The legacy auto-hiding overlay mode is preserved only when `overlay_strip = true` is explicitly set.
  - **`crates/render/src/snapshot.rs` & `crates/render/src/tests.rs`**: Updated `PlayerSnapshotSelect::Hidden` to set `frame.overlay_strip = true` before `hide_strip()`, and updated `lightbox_draws_a_dark_backdrop_over_the_base` to sample inside `frame.slide_rect()` rather than assuming the slide fills the letterbox corners.
  - **`docs/spec/triggers.md`**: Updated the Player chrome section to document the default reserved-strip layout and explicit `overlay_strip` opt-in.
- **Tests added:**
  - `coursecraft-render` (`crates/render/src/player_frame/tests.rs`): `reserved_strip_never_overlaps_slide_across_aspects_and_sizes` (16:9, 4:3, portrait across wider, taller, exact, tiny, and fullscreen sizes; verifies non-intersection, aspect ratio, video rect containment, captions, and persistence after idle), `rendered_bottom_edge_textbox_is_fully_visible_above_bar`, and `overlay_strip_explicit_opt_in_auto_hides_and_uses_full_area`.
